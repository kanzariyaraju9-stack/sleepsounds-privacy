Sleep Sounds & Alarm Clock

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026 · App ID: com.rkstudio.sleepsounds

In short

  • We don't ask for your name, email or account, and the app has no sign-up.
  • Your alarms, mixes, settings and sleep history stay on your phone.
  • We don't show ads, use analytics or tracking tools, or sell or share your data.
  • Payments for Pro and app ratings are handled by Google Play.

This policy explains how the Android app "Sleep Sounds & Alarm Clock" (the "App"), published by FocusCore Labs ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App has no account or login, and it does not send what you do in the App to us or to any server we run.

Information stored on your device

To work, the App saves the following only on your phone:

  • Your alarms and their settings (time, days, label, wake-up challenge, sound, snooze and vibration).
  • Your saved sound mixes, volumes and sleep-timer choices.
  • Your sleep history: the times you started and stopped sleeping and how often you snoozed, used to build your sleep report.
  • App settings such as bedtime reminder, time format, animations, chosen app icon and whether you own Pro.
  • Small counters that decide when to show the "rate the app" message.

You can delete your sleep history at any time in Settings › Clear sleep history. Uninstalling the App removes all of this data from your phone.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this App data in your device backup. That backup is controlled by Google and your device settings, not by us, and we cannot access it.

Purchases (Pro)

Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your card or payment details. Google tells the App only whether you own Pro, so the App can unlock Pro features. Google's handling of payments is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Ratings and reviews

If you choose to rate the App, the rating window is shown by Google Play (In-App Review). Your rating and review go to Google Play and are public there under Google's rules. We do not receive any extra information through this.

Permissions the App uses

  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms): to ring your alarm at the exact time you set.
  • Notifications and full-screen alerts: to show the alarm screen and the "sounds playing" notification.
  • Run at startup: to set your alarms again after the phone restarts.
  • Foreground service (media playback) and keep awake: to keep sleep sounds and alarms playing while the screen is off.
  • Vibration: for alarms that vibrate.

The App does not use your microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos. If you pick one of your phone's ringtones as an alarm sound, the App only stores a reference to that ringtone on your phone.

Third-party services

The only outside services the App uses are Google Play Billing and Google Play In-App Review, both provided by Google. The App contains no advertising and no analytics or crash-reporting tools.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Data security

Your data stays in the App's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. Because we do not collect or transmit your data, there is nothing stored on our side that could be exposed.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Important changes will also be mentioned in the App's update notes.
